FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Hotspot slow down my website or web app?
No. The hotspot.js runtime weighs just 3.8KB (under 1/100th the weight of Hotjar or GA4) and executes completely asynchronously with 0ms DOM blocking time. It records vector delta physics rather than streaming heavy canvas videos.
Do I need technical or data engineering skills to use Hotspot?
Not at all. You paste one line of script into your site's <head> tag once. From that moment forward, Hotspot autocaptures all routes, clicks, and rage taps, then provides plain-English instructions and 1-click prompts for Cursor or Claude.
How is Hotspot different from Google Analytics 4?
GA4 tells you that 70% of your visitors left, but gives you zero clues as to why. Hotspot identifies the exact broken button, rage tap cluster, mobile overlap, or hydration failure causing users to drop off, and gives you the exact code to fix it.
Does it work on custom Next.js, Vite, and mobile web apps?
Yes. Hotspot is framework-agnostic and runs on Next.js App Router, React/Vite, Remix, Astro, Webflow, Shopify, WordPress, and custom SPAs.

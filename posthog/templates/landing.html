How our automated audit works

Three steps from dropping in one script to knowing exactly what to fix.

Step 01

Drop in one script tag

No npm installs. No custom telemetry schemas. Drop in one script tag and Hotspot automatically records user coordinates, clicks, scrolls, and journeys in real time.

Step 02

We audit the whole journey

Hotspot walks your app like a buyer from the landing page to checkout. It detects slow steps, unlinked buttons, dead links, rage clicks, and mobile layout dropoffs.

Step 03

Fix the biggest leaks first

Everything is compiled into one prioritized fix list with proof. Copy the ready-made prompt straight into Cursor or Claude Code, deploy the fix, and watch your score go up.

Frequently asked questions

How long does setup take?
Under 60 seconds. You copy our single script tag into your HTML <head>. Hotspot begins streaming visitor telemetry and building your conversion audit immediately.
Do I need data analytics or technical skills?
No. Hotspot is built specifically for non-analysts. Instead of overwhelming you with SQL queries or complex event filters, we give you a plain-English score and a ranked checklist of what to fix.
Can I use Hotspot with Cursor, Claude, or Bolt?
Yes! That is our signature superpower. Every single detected friction point comes with a one-click "Copy Prompt for AI" button so you can paste the fix directly into Cursor, Claude, or ChatGPT.
Does the script slow down my website?
Not at all. The Hotspot SDK is a lightweight ~12KB script that loads asynchronously without blocking page rendering. Total execution time is under 8ms.

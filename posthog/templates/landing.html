Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Hotspot

Does Hotspot slow down my website or store? +
No. The initial audit crawls your public pages from the outside, requiring zero code installation on your store. If you choose to install our optional live tracking script, it weighs just 3.8KB (under 1/100th the weight of Hotjar or GA4) and executes asynchronously with 0ms render blocking.
Do I need technical or coding skills to use Hotspot? +
Zero coding is needed. Every audit report is written in crystal-clear plain English with actionable steps you or your store manager can implement directly inside Shopify, WooCommerce, or Webflow settings.
How is Hotspot different from Google Analytics 4? +
Google Analytics 4 tells you that 80% of your visitors left, but never tells you why. Hotspot identifies the exact broken button, layout shift, or mobile UX issue that caused them to leave, and gives you a prioritized fix to win them back.
Does it work on custom stores or SaaS websites? +
Yes! While optimized for e-commerce (Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce), Hotspot works beautifully on any web application, SaaS landing page, Webflow project, or Next.js store.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time with a single click from your dashboard. Paid plans also come with a full 14-day money-back guarantee.
